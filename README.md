# AI-Powered Automatic Email Reply System

## What it does

An AI-powered email automation system that reads incoming emails, understands their content, and automatically generates and sends relevant replies.

## Problem it solves

Businesses spend hours reading and replying to emails every day. This workflow saves time by automating repetitive email responses, improving response speed, and helping businesses manage their inbox more efficiently.

## Tools used

n8n, OpenAI / Gemini, Gmail, Webhooks (optional)

## How to use it

1. Open n8n.
2. Click **Import from file**.
3. Choose the JSON workflow file from this repository.
4. Connect your Gmail account.
5. Add your OpenAI / Gemini API credentials.
6. Configure the email trigger and AI reply instructions.
7. Test the workflow with a sample email.
8. Activate the workflow to automate email replies.

## Key features

- Automatically detects incoming emails.
- Uses AI to understand email content and intent.
- Generates relevant, professional email replies.
- Supports automated email sending through Gmail.
- Reduces manual work and saves time.
- Can be customized for customer support, business inquiries, and general email management.

## Use cases

- Customer support email automation.
- Business inquiry responses.
- Appointment and booking inquiries.
- Frequently asked questions.
- General business email management.

## Note
Configure your own credentials and test the workflow before activating automatic replies. For sensitive emails, consider requiring human approval before sending responses.
